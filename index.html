Clock out. Floor it.

A kart racer where the track is your office and the kart is your chair. It runs in your browser, so technically you could play it on your lunch break. Technically.

01 · The track

The office is the track.

Open plan, canteen, server room, board room, reception. Then out onto the rooftop car park at sunset and down the ramp into the garage, past the colleagues who stayed to watch. One lap, every room.

02 · The handling

Drift like you’re late for a meeting.

If it has castors, it drifts. Hold Space and the longer you hold it, the harder you boost. Hit the pallets and the same key turns into a flip. Land on the backrest and spend the next corner thinking about what you did.

03 · The weapons

Mandatory Security Training.

The cruellest thing in the piñata. It homes in on whoever is ahead of you and stops them dead until they pass a quiz on phishing emails and suspicious USB sticks.

Also in the break room: the Fire Extinguisher, a Coffee Spill, Der Pömpel, and Fish in the Microwave, which clears the whole floor.

Made for people with a desk.

No install. No IT ticket.

It runs in a browser tab. No admin rights, no gaming PC, no asking anyone for permission.

Fits between two meetings.

A race is three laps and about five minutes. Lunch break, slow Friday, the bit before the all-hands.

It’s just a tab.

Somebody walks past your screen? Close it. You were checking the train times.

One code for the whole team.

Drop the room code in the team chat. Five of you on the grid, and finally a fair way to decide who gets the window desk.

The prize. One mug.

World’s Okayest Employee. It has lived in the third kitchen longer than anyone has worked here. Nobody knows whose it was. Nobody has ever washed it.

Win the race and it sits on your desk until next Friday. Then all six of them come for it again.
